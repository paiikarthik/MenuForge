        The Economics of Open Innovation

        Why Open-Weight Models Win For Restaurant Menus

        Independent restaurants operate on 3% to 9% margins. A side-hustle charging $49 setup or $15/month cannot afford SaaS token runaway, unpredictable rate limits, or sending proprietary family recipes off-premise.

        Closed Cloud APIs (OpenAI / Claude / Google)
        • Flattens cultural cuisine: Generic tokens translate Kori Gassi to “chicken curry” and Mole Poblano to “chocolate stew”, stripping away heritage.
        • Metered API costs: $0.03 – $0.08 per token call plus image upload costs eat into your $15/mo subscription margins.
        • Basement kitchen downtime: Restaurants often have spotty cell reception and dead-zone basement prep kitchens. If WiFi drops, SaaS breaks.
        • Confidentiality risk: Proprietary spice ratios and plating secrets are shipped to third-party cloud servers.
        Local Open Weights (MenuForge + Ollama)
        • Heritage preservation: Keeps native proper nouns intact; crafts nuanced bilingual culinary copy with phonetic pronunciation.
        • $0 marginal cost: Run 100 or 10,000 seasonal dish updates on your laptop without paying a cent in API tokens.
        • 100% offline reliability: Operates at full speed on 127.0.0.1 with zero internet required.
        • Absolute IP privacy: Family recipes, heirloom notes, and unreleased seasonal dishes stay safely inside the kitchen.
